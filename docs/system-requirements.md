# Phase 1 - Functional Requirements (MVP)

## Objective

Build a quadcopter capable of stable, manually controlled flight while establishing the core hardware and software architecture for future enhancements.

## Functional Requirements

- The drone shall arm and disarm safely under operator command.
- The drone shall take off from the ground under manual control.
- The drone shall maintain a stable hover under manual control.
- The drone shall respond to roll, pitch, yaw, and throttle inputs.
- The drone shall translate forward, backward, left, and right while maintaining controlled flight.
- The drone shall land safely under manual control.
- The drone shall support an emergency stop that immediately disarms the motors.
- The drone shall report telemetry to the ground station.

## Exit Criteria

The MVP shall be considered complete when the following conditions are satisfied:

- [ ] The drone powers on successfully.
- [ ] The drone arms and disarms correctly.
- [ ] All four motors rotate in the correct direction.
- [ ] The drone successfully takes off.
- [ ] The drone maintains a stable hover.
- [ ] The drone can translate in all horizontal directions under operator control.
- [ ] The drone lands safely.
- [ ] The emergency stop immediately disarms the motors.
- [ ] Telemetry is successfully received by the ground station.
